DinkHub - Privacy & Terms

Last updated 22 July 2026

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what DinkHub collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address, and the profile details you provide (year of birth, gender, mobile number, town/city, country, a profile photo, an optional bio, and an optional DUPR ID). As you use the app we store your club memberships, event participation, match results, and an internal per-club skill rating.

How we use it

We use your information to run your clubs and events: to show who is attending, generate fair games, record results, calculate internal ratings, and send you notifications about events, invitations, and waitlist offers. We do not sell your personal information.

What other members can see

Members of a club you join can see your name and profile photo. Your internal rating is private and never shown to other players. Your email and phone number are not shared with other members through the app.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, we register a device token to deliver push notifications. You can turn reminders off and manage preferences in Notification Settings, or disable notifications for the app in your device settings.

Data storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (Postgres, authentication, and file storage). Access is controlled by row-level security so members only see what they are permitted to.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from Profile > Delete Account. If you own a club, you will be asked to transfer or delete it first.

Contact

Questions about your data? Contact us at dinkhubappsupport@gmail.com.
