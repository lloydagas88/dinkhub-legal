Delete your DinkHub account

Last updated 13 August 2026

DinkHub is developed by Lloyd Agas. You can delete your account and its data yourself, from inside the app, at any time. You do not need to contact us.

How to delete your account

  1. Open DinkHub and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Type your password to confirm. This step exists so that nobody else can delete your account from an unlocked phone.

Deletion happens immediately. It cannot be undone, and we cannot restore an account once it has been deleted.

If you own a club

The app will refuse to delete your account while you still own a club, and will name the clubs concerned. This is deliberate: deleting a club owner would delete the whole club along with every member's events, results and history. Transfer ownership to another member, or delete the club, and then delete your account.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, username, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender, town or city, country, profile photo, bio and DUPR ID.
  • Your club memberships.
  • Your event signups and waitlist places.
  • Your match participation and results, and your internal skill ratings.
  • Your notifications, notification preferences and push notification tokens.
  • Any fixed doubles pairings you were part of.

What is kept, with your name removed

A small amount of information stays so that clubs you were part of are not damaged by your leaving. In each case the link to you is removed, so it is no longer personal information about you:

  • Events you organised remain, so the club keeps its history. The organiser field is emptied.
  • Announcements you posted remain, with the author field emptied.
  • Guest players you added remain on their events, with the record of who added them emptied.

Crash reports

If the app has ever crashed for you, a diagnostic report may have been sent to Sentry, our error reporting provider. These reports are configured not to include personally identifying information, are stored in the European Union, and are automatically discarded after Sentry's retention period. They are not linked to your account and are not affected by deleting it.

Backups

Deleted data may persist in encrypted database backups for a short period before those backups expire. It is not accessible through the app or to other members during that time.

Questions

If you cannot access the app to delete your account, or you want to check that a deletion has completed, email dinkhubappsupport@gmail.com from the address on the account and we will action it for you.
